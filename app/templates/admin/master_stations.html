Stations

The station module (MSMM) is the reference for a station: names, positions and codes. Corrections are made in the module, not here. VIATOR's own list, imported from the Trainline open dataset (ODbL), is the fallback when the module does not answer, and the key of VIATOR's internal joins. A search shows at most 10 stations.
